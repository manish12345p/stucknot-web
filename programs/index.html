The Member Journey

A clear path from learning fundamentals to contributing real value to the ecosystem.

PHASE 01

Apprentice

Focus on absorbing knowledge. Complete foundational curriculum and small, constrained projects to prove technical baseline.

PHASE 02

Contributor

Move from theory to practice. Submit pull requests to community tools, fix bugs, and begin collaborating with Builders.

PHASE 03

Builder

Propose and lead new initiatives. Architect solutions, mentor Apprentices, and hold voting rights on community direction.

PHASE 04

Maintainer

Maintain the ecosystem. Manage infrastructure, review major architectural changes, and ensure the long-term health of the network.
