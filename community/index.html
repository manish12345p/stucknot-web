Operating Principles

MANDATE 01

SHIPPING > SPECULATING

We don't do 'thought leadership' here. We write code, we design interfaces, we deploy systems. If you aren't pushing commits or sharing deliverables, you aren't participating. Progress is measured in shipped artifacts, not elaborate plans.

MANDATE 02

CRITIQUE IS A GIFT

Honesty accelerates growth. We expect members to tear down ideas rigorously and rebuild them stronger. Ego is left at the door; the work is all that matters.

The Rituals

How we structure our collaboration to guarantee output.

01
⬢

Mentorship Pairing

New builders are aggressively paired with veterans based on stack and ambition, not seniority. This is a 1:1 apprenticeship model focused entirely on unblocking technical hurdles during the first 30 days.

02
⚯

Project Assignment Flow

Tasks aren't claimed; they are assigned based on capacity and required velocity. Our open Kanban board acts as the single source of truth. If a ticket sits idle for 48 hours, it's reassigned.

03
▣

Code Review Culture

PRs are treated as educational artifacts. We require detailed context, architecture decisions, and failing tests before a review begins. Approvals require two independent peer sign-offs.

04
📢

Weekly Demos

Every Friday at 16:00 UTC. Live, unpolished demonstrations of what was built that week. No slide decks allowed. You show the working software or you pass your turn.
